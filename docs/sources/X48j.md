### D220. The pieces inside the item's box are the item; the food mask is cut to the box + 5%
- https://dev.meta.ai/docs/sam/reading-segmentation — SAM returns one record (box + mask) per instance; for a concept like "tomatoes" that is one per piece. The rule has no list of food types: every instance whose box is at least 70% inside the item's box is kept, and the instance that matches the box best (IoU at least 0.3) too; the item is accepted when its mask fills at least 30% of the box.
- The item's box comes from the scan (D218); the food mask is cut to that box plus 5% on each side, so a neighbour inside the same SAM mask is cut away. A container (D216) is not cut.

### D236. The label report keeps every change in order, how it was made, the chat lines and the photo choice
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155 — learning from human feedback: the user's corrections of a model's output are the training and evaluation signal; the change and its kind (by hand, by voice, by text, Undo) say which step was wrong.
- https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/guides/rules-of-ml — rule 11 and on: log the features and the outcome at serving time, so the training data is what the user saw ("training-serving skew").
- Typing in a field is one edit (the first value and the last), not one per key. At most 64 edits and 16 chat lines (500 bytes each); the report is at most 32 KB (was 16 KB).

### D237. The pickup text is never kept in the report
- https://gdpr-info.eu/art-5-gdpr/ — data minimisation: the pickup text can be an address; the report keeps only that the pickup changed and how. The listing keeps the pickup, as before.
- Independent review: a chat line that named a pickup (changed or not) can hold the address in its words: the app sends no words for it ("pickup": true) and the service keeps none.

### D238. The report always fits the 32 KB limit
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/TextEncoder — the app counts UTF-8 bytes, as the service does (a 500-character line in Japanese is 1500 bytes). Too large: the oldest chat lines go first, then the last edits; the item labels always stay (independent review).

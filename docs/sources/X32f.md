# X32f — sources for each decision

Change: X32f, the shutter answers at once. On the web the press keeps the shutter down (press opacity + a new `press.scale`) and stops the preview at once; the "Use this photo?" sheet opens as soon as the photo exists, with no resize before it; the resize to the upload size runs once, inside the Analyzing timer, after "Use photo". The preview stream is asked for the upload size, not the sensor size. A barcode read takes the same path. Decisions by T9, 2026-10-27: "user presses the button, then button visually shows that it was pressed, then analyzing pop up starts, then shrinking … user should not be waiting for confirmation of feedback ever"; keep the confirm step; barcode the same as the shutter; press opacity + shrink. Read 2026-10-27.

Own evidence:
- The X32e capture field from T9's phone (2026-10-27 22:21 UTC): `"source":"photo","ms":787,"w":3000,"h":4000,"prep_ms":10403`, preview `3000x4000`; T9 pressed the shutter about 6 times before it answered (the app ignores presses while a photo is in progress, and the photo stayed in progress during the 10.4 s resize).
- The real web export in Chromium with a fake camera: preview 2376x1337 (was 3840x2160); shutter while pressed: opacity 0.85, scale 0.9; preview paused 30 ms after the release; the sheet opened 0.6–1.1 s after the press; resize 330 ms (prepare(): 1,191 ms in X32e); upload 2376x1337; the 9 recorded-answer scenarios of X32b pass with no page errors.

### D63. One decode and one encode to the upload size, inside the Analyzing timer
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/createImageBitmap — decodes an image Blob (with its orientation, `imageOrientation` "from-image").
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/CanvasRenderingContext2D/imageSmoothingQuality — "high" for the downscale.
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLCanvasElement/toBlob — one JPEG encode at the D28 quality (0.9).

### D64. The press is answered at once
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLMediaElement/pause — the preview stops on the frame the user saw at the press.
- https://m3.material.io/foundations/interaction/states/overview — a pressed state is shown while the control is pressed; the shutter also stays pressed while the photo is taken.
- New token `press.scale = 0.9` (style guide line in the same change, rule 0).

### D65. The preview stream at the upload size, not the sensor size
- https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/MediaStreamTrack/applyConstraints — `ideal` width/height; the still photo (`ImageCapture.takePhoto`) does not depend on the preview size.
- `streamTarget` shrinks the camera's reported maximum to 2376 px on the long edge, aspect kept (tested); a frame fallback is still the upload size.

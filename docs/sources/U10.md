# U10 — sources for each decision

Change: U10 (branch `ui`), the role sheet.

### D1. The role sheet rises over the screen on view

The earlier app showed the roles as a medium sheet over the sign-in screen right after sign-in, and over the doodles at start; T9 (Oct 29) confirmed this ("it pops up from below the screen once the sign in is successful").

- Material Design 3, bottom sheets: https://m3.material.io/components/bottom-sheets/overview

### D2. Words, made simpler

T9 (Oct 29) agreed the words "What do you want to do?", "Share your food", "Find food near you", "Log out".

- ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: https://www.asd-ste100.org/

### D3. A dialog on a wide screen

Medium and expanded widths show a centred dialog in place of a bottom sheet (the window size classes of `layoutFor`).

- Material Design 3, window size classes: https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/applying-layout/window-size-classes

### D4. Closing the sheet

From ☰ the sheet closes with a tap on the dim layer or Esc on the web; right after sign-in and at start it does not close (a role is needed).

- WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices, modal dialog (Escape closes): https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/

### D5. The role on view is marked

The card of the role on view has the accent border, and `aria-selected` tells a screen reader.

- WAI-ARIA 1.2, aria-selected: https://www.w3.org/TR/wai-aria-1.2/#aria-selected

### D6. react-native 0.88.1

Expo SDK 58 now lists react-native 0.88.1 (react-native 0.88.1 was published Oct 27; expo 58.0.21, Oct 28, bundles it), and `expo install --check` stops a change on 0.88.0. The pin goes to 0.88.1; the lock file changes only the react-native packages.

- npm, react-native versions: https://www.npmjs.com/package/react-native?activeTab=versions
- Expo, expo install --check: https://docs.expo.dev/more/expo-cli/#install

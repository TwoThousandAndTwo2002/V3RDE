# X47a — sources for each decision

Change: X47a, the voice service sends keywords the transcription accepts (`post/voice.go`, `post/voice_test.go`, `docs/morpheus/post_api.md`; NEW_WORK.md 47).

Own evidence (2026-10-29, the live site after X46b, DuckDuckGo 26.5.2 on macOS, read with x46c_voicelog.sh from the box events):
- 20:54 and 20:57 UTC: `POST /api/voice` 200 (1 item, 13 items; transcripts "Six trays of pasta.", "two three one nine South Perry Street Denver Colorado").
- 21:00:41 and 21:00:54 UTC: 502 `upstream_error` "the transcription failed" in 444 and 249 ms at the edge. The call `voice:transcribe` (muse-voice-transcribe-1.0) answered HTTP 400 `invalid_request_error` "ASR keyword 3 exceeds the maximum length of 30 characters".
- The service sent every item title (up to 120 bytes, `VoiceMaxTitle`) as one keyword. The app shows the generic voice error for a 502.
- The same app, browser and microphone made a good clip in the voice check (6.9 s, peak 0.958, RMS 0.070, 200 from the service), so the capture is not the cause.

### D195. Keywords of at most 30 characters, cut at a word end
- https://github.com/meta-models/meta-model-cookbook — `keywords` is an array of strings in the `request` part (the shape of X35d).
- https://github.com/praneeth-pra/hi-chat/pull/94 — the same 400 ("ASR keyword … exceeds the maximum length of 30 characters") from another Muse integration; its fix leaves out the long terms.
- Here a long title is cut at the last word end before 30 characters, because its first words ("Roasted Vegetable and Chickpea") still help the recognition; only a title with no word end before 30 is left out. Characters are counted as Unicode code points. Equal keywords (any case) go once. No limit on the number is known: a live clip with 13 keywords passed.

### D196. The events line counts the keywords
- https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/ — record what a request sent, so a refusal can be traced without a new test.
- `detail.keywords` (how many were sent) and `detail.keywords_cut` (how many titles were cut or left out).

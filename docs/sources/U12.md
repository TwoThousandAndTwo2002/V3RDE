# U12 — sources for each decision

Change: U12 (branch `ui`), the Receive screen.

### D1. A photo-first feed in columns

The earlier app showed the listings photo first, in 1, 2 or 3 columns by window width, with Grab on the card. The columns follow the window size classes.

- Material Design 3, window size classes: https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/applying-layout/window-size-classes

### D2. Pages by cursor

The listings service gives one page and the cursor of the next (`?after=`); the app adds a page when the list is near its end and shows no listing two times.

- V3RDE listings service (branch services): https://github.com/TwoThousandAndTwo2002/V3RDE/tree/services/listings

### D3. Plain links after a grab

Maps and WhatsApp are plain links (a map search URL and wa.me), no SDK in the app.

- Google Maps URLs: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started
- WhatsApp, click to chat: https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934

### D4. A close button on the sheet

The grab sheet has a ✕, and a tap on the photo, Esc or Back closes it (the earlier review: people did not know the tap above the sheet).

- WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices, modal dialog: https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/

### D5. Pull to refresh on a phone

- React Native, RefreshControl: https://reactnative.dev/docs/refreshcontrol

Change: X35b, the rescue service (`post/rescue.go`, `cmd/rescue`, port 8089). A listing that nobody claimed before its timer ran out: Muse Spark 1.3 looks at the listing photo, decides shelf-stable or perishable and writes an inquiry; the Feed America directory gives the nearest food banks (shelf-stable) or meal programs (perishable); the donor reads the draft and sends it. Build only: no compose or Caddy route yet. Read 2026-10-28.

Own evidence: 11 Go tests (a fake directory, Muse and mail relay: the routing by kind, the owner and timer rules, one rescue per listing, the failure paths, the relay's header, address, rate and per-day rules, a relay that never answers, the store). No live call yet.

### D76. The Feed America directory: free, no key, by ZIP or by location
- https://feedam.org/developer-portal — `GET /api/resources/search?zip=&mode=` and `/api/resources/nearby?lat=&lng=`, no auth, results in `results`, 10,000 requests per day per IP; `mode=free` = pantries, soup kitchens and mobile pantries; CC BY 4.0 with the credit "Feed America (feedam.org), CC BY 4.0".
- https://feedam.org/ai-answers — Feed America is not Feeding America (two separate charities).

### D77. Shelf-stable food → food banks; perishable food → meal programs
- https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank — a food bank is a warehouse that stores donated food; a pantry gives it to people.
- https://www.fsis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media_file/2020-08/Product-Categorization.pdf — USDA FSIS: "FSIS does not require shelf stable products to be frozen or refrigerated for food safety purposes"; Muse decides from the photo with that rule (sealed food that keeps without a refrigerator).

### D78. The donor sends; nothing is sent without the donor
- https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6068 — the mailto: URI with subject and body, so the donor's own mail app sends the draft.
- https://feedam.org/api/openapi.json — no result field for an email that we could confirm, so the donor gives the address, or calls (tel: links).

### D79. The optional relay: STARTTLS, the donor as Reply-To, no header injection, 3 per day
- https://pkg.go.dev/net/smtp@go1.25.2 — SendMail "switches to TLS if possible"; "PlainAuth will only send the credentials if the connection is using TLS or is connected to localhost".
- https://shoulder.dev/threats/cwe/CWE-93 — CWE-93 CRLF injection: CR LF in a header value can "add hidden recipients (Cc/Bcc injection)"; for Go, "reject input containing CRLF characters and validate email addresses". CR and LF are removed from headers; addresses are checked with net/mail.
- https://owasp.org/API-Security/editions/2023/en/0xa4-unrestricted-resource-consumption/ — one rescue per listing, one email per place, 3 per donor per day, a rate limit; a place with a known address gets email only at that address, so the relay cannot send to any address.
- https://pkg.go.dev/net@go1.25.2 — `DialTimeout` and `SetDeadline`: the SMTP exchange has one time limit (SendMail has none), so a silent relay cannot hold a request.

### D80. Only the donor's own listing, still active, after its timer ran out
- https://owasp.org/API-Security/editions/2023/en/0xa1-broken-object-level-authorization/ — the listings service decides ownership (`/api/listings/mine` with the donor's cookie); one 404 for unknown and not-owned.

### D81. The draft does not say the food is safe
- https://www.congress.gov/117/bills/s5329/BILLS-117s5329es.htm — the Food Donation Improvement Act covers "apparently wholesome food" donated in good faith; the app does not make a safety claim for the donor, and the food program decides.

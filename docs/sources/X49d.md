### D252. The scanner's details are shown, not thrown away
- X48d kept them with each listing (`listings/details.go`); T9 (2026-10-30): "we need to use these results rather then just through them out."
- https://m3.material.io/components/bottom-sheets/overview — a bottom sheet for supplementary content on a phone, so the card does not grow; on a wide screen a side column (a list-detail layout: https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/canonical-layouts/list-detail).
- The cost is never shown (T9). The third column only when the window holds the wide dialog (1140 + 2 x 16 pt); below that the list would be too narrow, so Details opens the sheet, as on a phone.
- Independent review: a details change ends the chat's Undo (Undo would remove it); Change is locked while the listings post; Done with no change keeps the weight as it was; the Details pill stays after the user clears the details (to add them back); the open details close when their item is removed; an item removed during a post does not come back when its post fails.

### D253. The text types in, and stops for "reduce motion"
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion — with reduce motion on (the setting the doodles already read), the text shows at once and the box does not grow.

### D254. A change of the details is recorded, as a change of the title and the servings
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155 — the user's corrections of a model's output are the feedback signal (X48u, D236): every change of a field that a model made is in the label report, for self-improvement. The four new field names are condition, diet, portions and weight; the service takes them (`labelFields`).

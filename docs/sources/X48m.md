### D224. One rule picks the mask by the scene: the spill of the words mask past the item's box
- No food list (T9: "as general as possible"): the rule reads only what the scan and SAM give. A whole-photo item has no box: words (it keeps the container, D216). A words mask that stays inside the item's box + 5% is one object (a package, a plate): words. A words mask with more than 3% of its pixels outside that box, or no words mask, is a pile that SAM joined with its neighbour: the box mask (D221), the established Grounded-SAM step (https://pyimagesearch.com/2026/01/19/grounded-sam-2-from-open-set-detection-to-segmentation-and-tracking/).
- The 3% limit is a first value; the page shows each photo's spill so T9 can set it from real scans.
- Step 2 (https://arxiv.org/html/2403.04194, SAM-PD not-this points) is not used: on the X48l page it cut holes in the items, as the paper warns ("negative point prompts may occasionally lead to unstable mask predictions").

# U14 — sources for each decision

Change: U14 (branch `ui`), the camera and the scan.

### D1. The layout of the approved mockups

The camera and the sheets follow the approved X25 scan mockup and the X32 mockup, measured in U5.

- U5 parts review (the X25 and X32 states): https://claude.ai/artifact/42WvX7iM3A8Bcc73AFtiUL

### D2. The camera and barcodes

expo-camera gives the live picture, the photo and the barcode read (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E: the codes the catalog knows).

- Expo, Camera: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/camera/

### D3. Upload and drag-and-drop

expo-image-picker opens the photo library; on the web an image can also be dropped on the window.

- Expo, ImagePicker: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/imagepicker/
- MDN, HTML drag and drop: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTML_Drag_and_Drop_API

### D4. The upload size

The photo is made a JPEG with the long edge at most 2376 px at quality 0.9 (logic.ts UPLOAD, X32 D28); the size comes from the decoded image.

- Expo, ImageManipulator: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/imagemanipulator/

### D5. One sheet for each answer code

The analyze route gives a fixed code for each answer that is not a result (`not_food`, `model_no_food_single`, `model_no_food_multi`, `already_listed`, `daily_cap_reached`, retry codes); each has its sheet, and the photo stays.

- V3RDE analyze server (branch services): https://github.com/TwoThousandAndTwo2002/V3RDE/tree/services/server

### D6. The camera opens by itself

When the giver has nothing up, Give opens the camera at once (the earlier review); with a listing up, the list shows first.

- Nielsen Norman Group, reduce interaction cost: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/interaction-cost-definition/

# U5 — sources for each decision

Change: U5 (branch `ui`), the design tokens, the shared UI parts and the style guide of V3RDE.

### D1. The look is measured from the mockups approved during the Hackathon

The values come from the shots of the X32 mockup (approved by T9 on Oct 27 2026) and, for the camera and the small sheets that X32 does not show, from the X25 scan mockup (Oct 25 2026). `mobile/tools/ui/measure_x32.py` reads the X32 shots along fixed scan lines (phone 3 px = 1 pt, desktop 2 px = 1 pt) and writes `mobile/tools/ui/x32_measure.json`. Text sizes are glyph heights divided by the glyph heights in the Poppins font file (fontTools).

- X32 mockup (approved): https://claude.ai/artifact/6AMxFSoWfFfGMySXPefUKW
- fontTools, glyph bounds: https://fonttools.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ttLib/tables/_g_l_y_f.html

### D2. Brand assets, labelled as pre-existing

Hungree red `#BC202C` and the Poppins font are Hungree LLC brand assets from before the Hackathon. Poppins is under the SIL Open Font Licence and comes from `@expo-google-fonts/poppins`.

- Poppins, licence: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins/license
- @expo-google-fonts: https://github.com/expo/google-fonts

### D3. Platform rules

Touch size 44 × 44 pt (the 38 pt button inside a review card has a 44 pt touch area). Text contrast 4.5:1 (the style test computes it in light and dark mode). Bottom sheets below 600 pt of width, dialogs from 600 pt (medium) and 840 pt (expanded).

- Apple HIG, accessibility (touch size): https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility
- WCAG 2.1, contrast (minimum): https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#contrast-minimum
- Material 3, window size classes: https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/applying-layout/window-size-classes

### D4. The shade over the photo, and the frosted layer

All 23 sheet and dialog shots have a black gradient over the photo: alpha 0 at the top, 0.15 at 40 % of the height, 0.7 at the bottom. The frosted layer is a blur of the photo with a white tint of 0.55 (a fit to the 23 shots). `expo-linear-gradient` draws the shade; `expo-blur` draws the blur.

- Expo, LinearGradient: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/linear-gradient/
- Expo, BlurView: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/blur-view/

### D5. Glyphs are SVG paths

The reference glyphs (✕ − + and upload) are strokes with soft edges and a width of about 1.67 pt. Glyphs made of Views and borders gave strokes from 0.98 to 1.94 pt, because the browser snaps boxes and border widths to whole pixels. SVG paths with round ends (react-native-svg, MIT) match the reference (stroke 1.67 pt; the upload head width the same).

- Expo, react-native-svg: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/svg/
- react-native-svg (MIT): https://github.com/software-mansion/react-native-svg
- CSS Values 4, snap a length as a border width: https://www.w3.org/TR/css-values-4/#snap-a-length-as-a-border-width

### D6. A sheet height in whole points

A large sheet is at most 85 % of the screen height, rounded to a whole point (717 of 844 pt), and a dialog at most 0.869 of the window height (782 of 900 pt). With a fractional sheet top, every part in the sheet sat between device pixels and the glyph strokes changed width.

- MDN, Window.devicePixelRatio: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/devicePixelRatio

### D7. Placeholder and text on the frost: the approved look (T9, Oct 29)

The approved placeholder colour `#9F9DA4` is 2.68:1 on white, and text on the frosted layer is 2.6–3.9:1 in the approved shots. T9 accepted both. Every field has a label above it, so the placeholder only gives an example.

- WCAG 2.1, contrast (minimum): https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#contrast-minimum
- WCAG 2.1, labels or instructions: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/labels-or-instructions.html

### D8. Values that the mockups do not show: choices (T9, Oct 29)

Press opacity 0.85, the row rise (8 pt, 40 ms apart, 220 ms), the sheet spring (speed 14, no bounce, from 0.2 of the window height), the pill padding 20 and the dark-mode colours are choices. The guide marks them "Choice". T9 accepted them.

- React Native, Animated: https://reactnative.dev/docs/animated

### D9. The style gate

`tests/style.test.mts` (8 tests, Node test runner) checks the parts and the app shell: no colour, font, text size or radius outside the tokens; no fractional number in a part or a screen; the same colour names in light and dark mode; text contrast 4.5:1; the guide lists every token of `theme.ts` with its value and no other token. The test was run with deliberate errors (a changed guide value, a removed guide row, a new token, a raw colour, radius and opacity in the shell); each one failed.

- Node.js, test runner: https://nodejs.org/api/test.html

### D10. Independent parity checks

Two independent agents measured the new shots against the 25 approved shots (edge scans, colour region means, OCR, glyph masks, a corner circle fit). The first found the missing shade and errors in colours, glyph strokes and spacing; the second found three more (the frost tint, the corner radius 26, the upload head). After the fixes, the layout is within 0.33 pt in 24 states and within 1.0 pt in one, and frost, shade and colours are within 2 levels.

- Playwright, browser contexts (viewport, deviceScaleFactor): https://playwright.dev/docs/api/class-browser#browser-new-context

### D11. Everything under `mobile/`

The style guide is `mobile/docs/style_guide.md` and the tools are in `mobile/tools/ui/`. Branch `ui` writes only `mobile/`, `NEW_WORK_UI.md` and `docs/sources/U*.md`, so it never changes a file that branch `services` changes.

- Git, git-worktree: https://git-scm.com/docs/git-worktree

### D12. Rewrite guard

The script carries the sha256 of every line (12 or more non-space characters) of the earlier `theme.ts`, `ui.tsx`, `style.test.mts` and `App.tsx` that the blame gives to the pre-kickoff snapshot. No line of the new files has one of them.

- Git, git-blame: https://git-scm.com/docs/git-blame

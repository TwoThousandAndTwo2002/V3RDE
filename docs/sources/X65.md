### D374. The language choice where the role changes, as an oval
- https://designsystem.digital.gov/patterns/select-a-language/three-or-more-languages/ — U.S. Web Design System, "Select a language": "Place the language selector menu at the top of the screen above site navigation" (top right for left-to-right languages), and each language in its own name. The phone's global menu is the role sheet; the wide screen's header is the top bar, so the pill is in both (it was only in the phone menu); in the top bar it is at the right, next to Log out. The name is the short native name (X49x).
- https://m3.material.io/components/buttons/guidelines — buttons of the same weight in one group look the same. In the role sheet the pill is the Log out pill's style (`Pill` secondary: `size.control`, `radius.round`, `card`, `line` border, the width of the sheet); in the top bar it is the top-bar Log out oval (`size.tabPadX`, `bg`, `muted` text, `iconFill` under the mouse). Only existing tokens. The sign-in row keeps the link (not asked for).
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/consistent-identification.html — the same function has the same name: every look keeps the one screen-reader name "Language: <name>". Test: "X65: the language choice is an oval pill where the role changes …".

### D375. A touch-up pick keeps More open
- https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/ — "Visibility of system status" and "User control and freedom": after a choice the user sees which photo is chosen (the red line) and can change it at once; closing the panel hid the choice and the other photo. T9's expected behaviour replaces the X50a decision (entry 96). The large photo of the one-item form still shows the photo that posts. Test: "X65: picking a photo keeps More open …" (no `closeMore()` or `setMoreOpen(` in the pick).

### D376. Two dialogs at once: the newest takes Esc and Tab
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/ — WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices, modal dialog: "When a dialog opens on top of another dialog", the new one is the active dialog; Escape "closes the dialog", and Tab stays in it. On a wide screen the dialogs of a role screen (the listing edit, Delete, Rescue, the pickup place) cover only the role panel, so the new top-bar oval can open the language sheet over one of them. Every `Popup` now joins a stack (`mobile/src/popupstack.ts`, new): only the newest takes Esc and keeps the Tab focus; the one under it gets them back when the newest closes. Before, one Esc closed both (the listing edit lost its changes). Test: "X65 review: two popups at once …".
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/min-width — a flex item's automatic minimum width is its content: the right group of the top bar has `minWidth: 0` and the oval can shrink, so a long language name or a long translation of Log out cuts its text and never pushes the role tabs.

Review (independent, 15 minutes at most): two findings, both fixed. (1) Two dialogs at once on a wide screen took one Esc together (D376). (2) A very long name could push the role tabs (D376, the min width). No defect found in: the language sheet from the top bar (it opens; no role sheet comes back after a pick; the focus goes back to the oval), the phone role sheet, the hover and press states next to Log out, Arabic (the row mirrors), More staying open (no new mount, the focus stays, the large photo still shows the photo that posts), the docs. The shots after the fixes are the same pixels as the approved mockup (top bar, role sheet, touch-up pick).

Own evidence: mobile tests 210 -> 212; the mockup's shots: "More open after the pick: false" before, "true" after, on phone and desktop (the Playwright run of both web builds).

### D275. Touch up and the details under one "More" button
- https://m3.material.io/components/bottom-sheets/overview — on a phone, supplementary content in a bottom sheet, so the card does not grow (as X49 D252).
- https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/ — progressive disclosure: the less used actions behind one control keep the list short; the details show at once when More opens (no second tap).

### D276. The chat in a frosted third column, 320 pt wide
- https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/canonical-layouts/supporting-pane — a supporting pane next to the main content on a wide window (the chat changes the list in the middle). 320 pt: at 260 the chat field cut its own hint ("Talk or type a chang…"); the frosted layer is the one of the sheets (T9: "the information box should be frosted").

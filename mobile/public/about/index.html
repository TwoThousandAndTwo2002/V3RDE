Posting food should take seconds

Sharing extra food used to mean typing each item, counting servings and taking a good photo. Most people gave up, and the food went to the trash. V3RDE uses Meta's AI models to do that work for you.

How it is built

Three layers. Our server does the quick checks and keeps your listings; Meta's AI models do the hard work. A red border is a Meta AI model that our server calls; Meta's PE-Core runs on our server.

Your phone or browser

  • Give, Receive and the chat
  • Camera and barcode reader
  • Microphone or keyboard
  • Seven languages

Our server

Front door

  • Sign-in and fair use
  • Food check (Meta's PE-Core)
  • Food DB for barcodes

Your food

  • Finds and counts each item
  • Finds an item it missed
  • A better photo
  • Changes by voice or text

Your listings

  • Live for 30 minutes
  • Grab and pick up
  • Rescue to a food program
  • Live updates in the web app

What we learn

  • Time and cost of each AI call
  • A daily report of donor changes
  • New text translated after each update

Meta's AI models

  • SAM 3.1 finds and counts
  • Muse Spark 1.3 names, understands, decides
  • Muse Image 1.0 makes the photo better
  • Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0 turns speech to text
  • Muse Spark Open translates

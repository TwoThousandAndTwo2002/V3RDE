Posting food should take seconds

Sharing extra food used to mean typing each item, counting servings and taking a good photo. Most people gave up, and the food went to the trash. V3RDE uses Meta's AI models to do that work for you.

One photo, not a form

Meta's models find, count and name each item, and make the photo look good.

Seven languages

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic and Yoruba.

Nothing left behind

Nobody grabbed it in 30 minutes? Rescue finds a food program near it.

How it is built

Three parts work together. Quick checks run on our own server; Meta's AI models do the hard work. Tap a flow below to see it step by step. A red border is a Meta AI model.

Your phone or browser

What you see and touch

  • Camera and barcode reader
  • Microphone or keyboard
  • The app in your language

Our server

Quick checks and your listings

  • Sign-in and fair use
  • Food DB and food check (PE-Core)
  • Listings, timers and Rescue
  • Checks every AI answer
  • Logs the time and cost of each call

Meta's AI models

The hard work

  • SAM 3.1: finds and counts
  • Muse Spark 1.3: names, understands, decides
  • Muse Image 1.0: better photos
  • Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0: speech to text
  • Muse Spark Open: translations
